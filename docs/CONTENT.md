## 7. FAQ `#faq`

H2 [verbatim]: **Frequently asked questions**. Las 8, literales:

1. **What types of cases do you specialize in?** We focus on high-aesthetic restorative dentistry, including veneers, full-mouth reconstructions, implant restorations, and digital smile design cases.
2. **Do you accept digital scans?** Yes. We work seamlessly with all major intraoral scanners and accept STL, DICOM, and Smilecloud® shared cases.
3. **How does your digital workflow improve case outcomes?** Our hybrid process blends precise digital modeling with hand-finished artistry, ensuring restorations that look natural, fit accurately, and photograph beautifully.
4. **What is your turnaround time?** Turnaround times vary by case type, but most aesthetic cases are completed in 10–14 days. Rush services may be available upon request.
5. **Can you collaborate directly with dentists during treatment planning?** Absolutely. We offer virtual case reviews, Smilecloud® share links, and one-on-one consultations to align treatment goals from the start.
6. **Do you offer try-ins or prototypes?** Yes. Digital mock-ups, 3D printed prototypes, and bisque try-ins are available to ensure accuracy before final fabrication.
7. **What materials do you work with?** We specialize in esthetic ceramics, including layered zirconia, lithium disilicate, and hybrid ceramic solutions. Material recommendations are tailored to each clinical scenario.
8. **How do I submit a new case?** You can send a case through: your intraoral scanner export · a Smilecloud® shared case · our online case submission form · direct file upload with photographs.
